# 0.1.0

## Added

- Practise nouns in their own study area with spelling exercises and flash cards.
- Practise noun endings in a Conjugations and forms exercise covering polite, short and te-forms.
- Practise hiragana and katakana characters in one Kana module, with remembered selections, nine fonts, review and high scores.
- Sync study progress between your phone and PC on the same trusted Wi-Fi network.
- Find sharing devices automatically, or switch to Manual to enter their address.
- Confirm matching codes on both devices to protect transfers from unwanted changes.
- Preview changes and choose to merge or replace your saved progress.
- Restore your previous progress if you need to undo an import.
- Study all opening vocabulary lists from the twelve GENKI I third-edition lessons, with each entry linked to its source lesson.
- Practise adverbs, expressions, pronouns, determiners, counters, conjunctions, particles, suffixes and general words together in Words & expressions.
- Mix saved vocabulary from multiple categories using toggles on the Words page, with read-only Known, Rehearsing and Training lists.

## Changed

- Vocabulary now belongs to its grammatical category, with learned, training and rehearsing progress preserved when words move.
- Select vocabulary by collapsible book and lesson groups, including whole-book or whole-lesson selection.
- Pairing, transfer progress and reviewing changes now open in popups. Close a popup to cancel.
- Connected devices are cleared from the search list so you can start fresh next time.
- More reliable progress saving, with protection against conflicting saves.
- The Words mixer offers eleven grammatical categories; General words remains in Words & expressions.
- Phone navigation scrolls horizontally so every destination keeps its complete, readable label.

## Fixed

- Verb action/change forms now use the correct endings without crashing for irregular verbs.
- Adjective te forms now handle na-adjectives and irregular adjectives correctly, including the grammar practice entries.
- Android Back moves through one page at a time instead of skipping the vocabulary selector.
- Study-list toggles expose their selected state correctly to assistive technology.

## Notes

- Update both devices before syncing. Older app versions cannot read newly saved progress.
- Transfer contents remain readable to others on the network; use a network you trust.
- Open **Home → Sync progress** to get started. See the [sync guide](../docs/local-sync.md) for help.
